# Contributing to Awesome Lichess

Suggest tools, articles, and other resources that are useful to the Lichess community and have a clear connection to Lichess. Free and paid resources are welcome. Please mention any costs or access limits.

Open an issue or pull request with the resource link and a short description of why it may be useful. Please check that it is not already listed. I review every suggestion and may edit entries for clarity, formatting, or fit.

By contributing original material, you agree to make it available under this repository's [CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](LICENSE). Linked projects, articles, and other third-party material retain their own licenses and rights.
